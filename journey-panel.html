Image provenance & how to read this journey

Supplier goods can be picked directly from storage in some operations. Damaged receipts go on hold. Returned goods become available again only after inspection and an inventory update. Humanoid tote handling is a narrow commercial use case—not evidence of a fully autonomous warehouse.
